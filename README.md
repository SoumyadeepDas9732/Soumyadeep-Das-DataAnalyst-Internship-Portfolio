## Task 4 — Data Storytelling & Statistical Validation

### Objective

Task 4 focused on converting analytical findings into a professional
business narrative and validating an analytical question using
statistical hypothesis testing.

### Hypothesis

The analysis tested whether average Total Sales differed significantly
between male and female groups.

### Statistical Method

**Welch's Independent-Samples t-test**

The hypotheses were:

**H₀:** Mean Total Sales is the same for male and female groups.

**H₁:** Mean Total Sales differs between male and female groups.

### Statistical Result

- Significance Level: **0.05**
- p-value: **0.6171**
- 95% Confidence Interval for Mean Difference:
  **₹-10,094.84 to ₹17,001.40**

Since the p-value was greater than 0.05, the analysis failed to reject
the null hypothesis.

The available data therefore did not provide sufficient statistical
evidence of a difference in average Total Sales between the male and
female groups.

### Key Skills

`Hypothesis Testing` `Welch's t-test` `Statistical Analysis`
`Confidence Intervals` `Data Storytelling` `Business Communication`

# 📈 Key Business Insights

The internship analysis produced several important findings:

1. High Value customers generated approximately **52.1% of total
   revenue**, despite representing only about **26% of unique
   customers**.

2. **Laptop** was the highest-performing product with
   **₹30,215,273.25** in revenue.

3. **Electronics** was the highest-performing category with
   **₹57,359,364.21** in revenue.

4. **Patna** generated the highest city-level revenue at
   **₹22,064,469.50**.

5. **March 2025** was the strongest month, generating
   **₹14,972,050.97**.

6. Statistical testing did not find sufficient evidence of a
   significant difference in average Total Sales between the male
   and female groups at the 5% significance level.

# 🎯 Business Recommendations

Based on the analysis:

- Prioritize retention of High Value customers.
- Develop Medium Value customers through targeted promotions and
  cross-selling.
- Maintain availability of high-performing products.
- Monitor the Electronics category closely.
- Track strong geographic markets such as Patna, Mumbai, and Kolkata.
- Monitor monthly KPI trends for inventory and promotional planning.
- Use statistical evidence alongside business context when making
  customer-targeting decisions.
