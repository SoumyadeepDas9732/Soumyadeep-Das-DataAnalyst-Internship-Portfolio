# Internship Learning & Professional Reflection

## ApexPlanet Data Analytics Internship

### Introduction

The ApexPlanet Data Analytics Internship provided me with practical
experience in completing an end-to-end data analytics project.

Throughout the internship, I progressed from understanding and
cleaning raw data to exploratory analysis, SQL, business intelligence,
customer segmentation, dashboard development, statistical validation,
and professional data storytelling.

---

## Task 1 — Data Wrangling

The first stage helped me understand the importance of data quality.

I learned how to inspect datasets, identify missing values and
duplicates, standardize data formats, detect potential outliers,
engineer useful features, and prepare an analysis-ready dataset using
Python and Pandas.

This task demonstrated that reliable analysis depends heavily on the
quality and structure of the underlying data.

---

## Task 2 — Exploratory Data Analysis & Business Intelligence

Task 2 helped me move from data preparation to analytical
problem-solving.

I used descriptive statistics, visualizations, SQL queries, and
business questions to identify patterns in customers, products,
categories, cities, and sales performance.

This task strengthened my understanding of how analysts convert
business questions into measurable queries and visual insights.

---

## Task 3 — Deep-Dive Analysis & Dashboarding

Task 3 introduced a more focused business analysis.

I defined core KPIs and performed customer segmentation based on
customer revenue contribution.

One of the strongest findings was that High Value customers generated
approximately 52.1% of total revenue while accounting for only about
26% of unique customers.

This demonstrated how segmentation can reveal patterns that may not
be immediately visible from overall sales statistics.

I also gained experience organizing KPIs and analytical findings into
a business dashboard.

---

## Task 4 — Statistical Validation & Data Storytelling

Task 4 helped me understand the importance of validating analytical
observations statistically.

I used Welch's independent-samples t-test to test whether average
Total Sales differed between male and female groups.

The resulting p-value was approximately 0.6171, which was greater
than the 0.05 significance level.

Therefore, the analysis failed to reject the null hypothesis and did
not provide sufficient evidence of a difference in average Total
Sales between the two groups.

This task improved my understanding of hypothesis testing, p-values,
confidence intervals, statistical interpretation, and communicating
results without overstating conclusions.

## Final Reflection

The most important learning from this internship was understanding
that data analytics is not limited to writing code or creating
charts.

A complete analytics workflow requires understanding the business
problem, preparing reliable data, selecting appropriate analytical
methods, interpreting results correctly, and communicating findings
in a way that supports decision-making.

The internship allowed me to apply these stages within a complete
project and strengthened my foundation for future work in data
analytics and data science.
